Dear Mama,

I could have written a normal Teacher's Day message. Something short. Something simple. Something that just says "Thank you for everything."

But honestly? That doesn't feel like enough when I'm writing this for you.

Because before you ever became the teacher your students know, you were already teaching me.

You taught me things that never needed a classroom. You taught me how to keep trying when something doesn't make sense. You taught me that being wrong doesn't mean I'm a failure. You taught me to listen, to be patient, to work hard, and to keep learning.

But more than that, you taught me how to be a person.

And I don't think I say thank you for that enough.

I know being a teacher isn't easy. There are lessons to prepare, students to guide, problems to solve, responsibilities to carry, and probably a hundred things that nobody else gets to see.

Yet somehow, after doing all of that, you're still Mama when you come home.

You're still the person who asks if I've eaten. The person who reminds me about things I forgot. The person who worries. The person who gives advice even when I don't ask for it. HAHAHA.

And honestly? I'm grateful for all of it.

Because one day, when I look back at my life, I know I'll realize that some of the most important lessons I ever learned weren't written on a board.

They came from you.

Thank you, Mama.

— Your student, your anak, forever ♡
